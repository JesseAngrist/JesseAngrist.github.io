## What's going on with me?

Well, in January 2026, a few great writers changed the course of my life, and the rest is commentary. In the last few months I've:

- invested myself into, and then (mostly) divested myself from a sort of Cartesian inquiry into moral philosophy, which I thought would clarify how I should live my life. It didn't. But I like reading about moral philosophy now!
- Picked up, dropped, picked up, and since entered a [shaky, uncertain relationship with a journaling habit...](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poetrymagazine/poems/40900/missed-time)
- Made mental but terribly infirm commitments to learn to sing and to sketch in my free time, and started training for a marathon. Cultivating virtue, baby!
- Fallen in love with the Western United States. [She smokes](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/25/nx-s1-5905334/wildfire-alert-in-the-western-u-s-reaches-its-highest-level), but she still [takes my breath away](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3654241/).
- Begun down a Path. Probably not The Path, though.

Some things I've come to regard as important and spent a lot of time thinking about recently are:
- What to do with education institutions in the age of AI?
- The design space for Good Lives and its unmapped wildernesses...
- Adult human development, in particular as described by Robert Kegan
- Aspirational (a la Callard) direction over one's activities and, therefore, one's entire life as the integral over those activities. I'm trying to flesh out my intuition that persons' ability to aspire to, and realize, new values and ends has direct, humongous ethical implications for how we allocate every single moment of our devastatingly brief lives!
- My original face, before even my parents were born
